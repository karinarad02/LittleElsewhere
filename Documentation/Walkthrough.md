# Walkthrough — contains ending spoilers

You begin in front of the cabin at (0, -4). Coordinates below use east as +X and north as +Y. The map shows the landmarks and your location; explore in any order.

1. Gather any five fallen wood piles: (6,-5), (-9,5), (12,3), (-15,10), (18,-10), (-19,-8), (29,5), (5,17).
2. Gather any three berry bushes: (-7,-6), (10,14), (-17,17), (19,-20), (-28,-12), (6,-16).
3. Stand near four different wildlife species and press E. Fox: near (11,-7); deer: (-13,13); rabbit: (-11,-11); squirrel: (24,17); bird: (-25,-19); owl: (-26,23). Animals wander within a small radius of these locations.
4. Read the letters near Glasswater Pond (15,11), the High Clearing (-20,19), and the Listening Grove (-21,-17).
5. Light the lanterns at Lantern Crossing (21,-17), the Listening Grove (-20,-13), and the High Clearing (-25,21).
6. At Glasswater Pond, approach the boat near (21,6) and press E. Press Space when the dark float crosses the central blue band. Catch two fish. Misses cost nothing.
7. Return to the cabin and press E. Once the journal objectives are complete, you fold a paper crane.
8. Carry it to the bench at the High Clearing (-22,20) and press E to release it.
9. Return to the cabin and press E for the ending. Choose to stay if you want to keep exploring.

The forest has no hard boundary. If you walk across one side of the map, you continue around the other side. There are no enemies or death states.

The new campsite is at (-8,-20), southwest of the cabin. Interacting rests for eight hours and changes the lighting/time; it is optional and does not consume supplies. The HUD displays day, time, phase, and active save slot. Begin at MainMenu, choose a slot, and wait for Loading before gameplay.
